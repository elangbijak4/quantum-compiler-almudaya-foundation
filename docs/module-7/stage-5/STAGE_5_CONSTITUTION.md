## 2. Constitutional Resolutions (Q1–Q30)

### Q1: What constitutes Stage 5 input?
A `ProviderNeutralExecutionResult` (or Stage 3 `SimulatorJobResult`), an explicitly identified reference distribution ($P_{\text{exact}}$ or $P_{\text{sampled}}$), and a versioned `StatisticalVerificationPolicy`.

### Q2: Who owns statistical verification authority?
Module 7 Stage 5 owns statistical result verification and Stage 11 lineage extension. It does NOT own compilation, lowering, local reference execution, or semantic equivalence certification.

### Q3: What are the three distinct probability result domains?
1. **$P_{\text{exact}}(x)$**: Exact statevector probability amplitudes ($|c_k|^2$) produced by ideal reference statevector execution.
2. **$P_{\text{sampled}}(x)$**: Finite-shot sampled probability distribution produced by finite-shot sampling of ideal statevector execution.
3. **$P_{\text{observed}}(x)$**: Observed probability distribution produced by finite-shot execution on a provider simulator or physical hardware backend.

### Q4: What statistical distance metrics are supported?
- **Hellinger Distance**:
  \[ H(P, Q) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \sqrt{ \sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \left( \sqrt{P(x)} - \sqrt{Q(x)} \right)^2 } \]
- **Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) Distance**: Cumulative distribution distance for discrete categorical bitstring outcome spaces.

### Q5: What are the verification decision states?
- `VERIFIED`: Observed statistical distance is strictly $\le$ policy threshold.
- `REJECTED`: Observed statistical distance strictly exceeds policy threshold under valid evidence.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: Insufficient shots ($N < N_{\text{min}}$), missing reference payload, ambiguous bit ordering, or numerical error.

### Q6: Is automatic re-execution or threshold adjustment permitted?
**NO.** Stage 5 MUST NOT automatically re-execute circuits, increase shots, modify random seeds, switch backends, or relax thresholds upon `REJECTED` or `INCONCLUSIVE` outcomes.

### Q7: How is Stage 11 historical lineage extended?
Stage 5 appends immutable verification events (`RESULT_VERIFIED`, `RESULT_REJECTED`, `RESULT_INCONCLUSIVE`) to the Stage 11 historical repository without rewriting past lineage records.

### Q8: What is the credential boundary for Stage 5?
Stage 5 MUST NOT process, store, or serialize raw API keys, tokens, or passwords. Only non-sensitive references (`credential_ref`) are preserved.

### Q9: Can Stage 5 modify native circuits or execution results?
**NO.** Stage 5 treats all input native circuits, provider programs, execution requests, and result payloads as 100% immutable.

### Q10: How are zero probabilities handled in statistical distance calculation?
Missing bitstrings in sparse distributions are explicitly assigned zero probability ($0.0$) in the joint outcome space $\mathcal{X} = \text{support}(P) \cup \text{support}(Q)$. Zero-probability values do NOT produce `NaN` or division-by-zero errors.
