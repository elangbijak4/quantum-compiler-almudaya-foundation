# MODULE 6 STAGE 10 — GLOSSARY & TERMINOLOGY

## Terminology Definitions

- **Audit Certificate**: Immutable governance artifact certifying the compliance, correctness, feasibility, and optimization status of a compiled quantum circuit.
- **Certification Level**: Governed status (`UNCERTIFIED`, `SEMANTICS_VERIFIED`, `FEASIBILITY_CERTIFIED`, `OPTIMIZATION_CERTIFIED`, `FULLY_GOVERNED_CERTIFIED`, `AUDIT_FAILED`).
- **Lifecycle Status**: Governed lifecycle state (`DRAFT`, `VERIFIED`, `DEPRECATED`, `PROMOTED`, `REJECTED`).
- **Governance Auditor**: Analysis-only component evaluating compliance across Stages 1–9.
- **Audit Finding**: Structured diagnostic log entry generated during governance evaluation.
